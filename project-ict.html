01 / OVERVIEW

More than a visual refresh. A complete digital foundation.

ICT Networks already had an established website and an existing search footprint, but the overall digital presentation no longer reflected the level of the company's IT services and technical expertise.

The project therefore went beyond a simple redesign. The objective was to modernize the user experience while improving the site's technical structure, search visibility, measurement capabilities and local online presence.

An important part of the work was preserving useful SEO equity from existing content while gradually building a cleaner, more modern and easier-to-manage web presence around it.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Modernize the brand without losing what already worked.

The existing website contained valuable content and search visibility, but the visual presentation, structure and digital measurement needed significant improvement.

04 / SEO PRESERVATION

Not every old page should be redesigned away.

One of the most important decisions was to preserve the existing Cisco equipment landing page. Although visually older than the new site, the page already had search visibility and was generating organic traffic.

Instead of deleting it or forcing it into the new site architecture, the page was carefully refreshed while retaining its original search value. Navigation, styling, metadata and technical SEO were improved, while its role as a dedicated landing page remained intact.

This became a good example of balancing redesign ambition with SEO reality: protect what performs, modernize what needs improvement and avoid changes that create unnecessary ranking risk.

07 / MY INVOLVEMENT

Hands-on ownership across design, implementation and SEO.

I handled the project as an end-to-end digital redesign rather than only a website styling exercise. That included the visual direction, page structure, HTML/CSS implementation, responsive adjustments and content presentation.

I also worked on the technical SEO layer — metadata, canonical URLs, structured data, Search Console, analytics, indexing and preservation of existing organic visibility.

The project became a practical combination of web design and digital strategy: improving how the company looks online while also improving how the site can be discovered, measured and developed further.
