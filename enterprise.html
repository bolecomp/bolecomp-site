WHAT I DO

From infrastructure decisions to service strategy.

Enterprise IT decisions rarely belong to a single technology domain. Compute, storage, virtualization, networking, security, operations, licensing and commercial constraints all influence the final solution.

My role is to connect those layers into an architecture that is technically sound, operationally realistic and aligned with the business objective behind the project.

HOW I WORK

Architecture is a process, not a diagram.

01
Understand

Clarify the business objective, current environment, constraints, risks and the real reason behind the technology request.

02
Architect

Define the target architecture, evaluate alternatives and balance technical quality with operational and commercial reality.

03
Validate

Use workshops, PoCs, design reviews and stakeholder alignment to reduce uncertainty before major implementation decisions.

04
Deliver

Keep architecture connected to implementation, migration, operations and the final business outcome.
