01 / OVERVIEW

Validate the cloud strategy before committing to the transformation.

The VMware Cloud Foundation PoC engagements were designed for enterprise customers considering a move from traditional virtualized infrastructure toward a modern private and hybrid cloud operating model.

The objective was not only to demonstrate individual VMware products, but to show how the complete software-defined data center stack could be deployed, operated and managed as an integrated platform — including compute virtualization, software-defined storage, network virtualization, lifecycle management, operations and automation.

The PoC environment provided a practical basis for architecture discussions, technical validation and future infrastructure planning before customers committed to production VCF deployments.

02 / BUSINESS DRIVERS

From traditional infrastructure to a cloud operating model.

04 / ENGAGEMENT MODEL

A hands-on PoC built around architecture, demonstration and testing.

Each engagement was structured as an intensive five-business-day workshop delivered by VMware-certified AIGO engineers using a four-node VCF proof-of-concept environment.

A consolidated architecture was used for the PoC, with management and tenant workloads sharing a workload domain and resource pools providing workload separation. The purpose was to reduce PoC complexity while still exposing the customer to the key architectural and operational concepts of VMware Cloud Foundation.

The workshops combined platform bring-up, architecture walkthroughs, operational demonstrations and customer testing, so the engagement functioned as both technical validation and consulting.
