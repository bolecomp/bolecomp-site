01 / OVERVIEW

A complete refresh of a business-critical data center platform.

The project covered the consolidation and technological modernization of the primary DDOR data center in Novi Sad. The existing infrastructure was replaced with a new compute, storage, SAN, Ethernet and virtualization platform, followed by migration of production virtual machines to the new environment.

Because the platform supports business-critical services, the project required careful architecture planning, staged implementation and a controlled migration strategy designed to preserve service reliability throughout the transition.

The final platform combined modern Fujitsu compute, HPE all-flash storage, high-speed Ethernet and Fibre Channel networking with VMware vSphere 8 and a dedicated Tanzu-oriented cluster.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Modernize the platform without compromising production continuity.

The project was not a simple hardware replacement. It required a new infrastructure architecture, integration with the existing environment, staged migration and validation of production workloads.

05 / MIGRATION

A staged transition from the legacy platform to the new data center.

Migration was treated as a separate architectural workstream rather than as the final step of installation. The new storage was integrated with both existing and new hosts so that workloads could be moved between environments in controlled phases.

The migration strategy combined storage migration, VM registration on the new vSphere environment and application-level validation. This approach provided a controlled path from the legacy VMware platform and HPE 3PAR storage to the new vSphere 8 and HPE Primera environment.

Each workload was validated after migration before proceeding with the next group, reducing operational risk during the transition of business-critical services.

I led the project from the initial presales phase through solution architecture, technology selection, detailed design and final implementation.

My role was to connect the customer requirements with the complete infrastructure architecture — compute, storage, SAN, Ethernet networking, VMware virtualization and migration strategy — and to keep those elements aligned throughout the delivery process.

During implementation, I coordinated the technical workstreams and the project team, followed the transition from design into the production environment and remained involved through migration and final validation.
